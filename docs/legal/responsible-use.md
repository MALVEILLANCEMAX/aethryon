# Responsible use

AETHRYON converts **public** media links into files. It is meant for content you **own** or are **allowed** to
download and reuse: your own uploads, content under a license that permits it (for example Creative Commons), content
whose rights holder gave you permission, or uses permitted by the law of your country.

## What AETHRYON does not do

- It does not host, index, search or recommend content.
- It refuses private videos, playlists and live streams, and does not handle DRM-protected media.
- It does not bypass paywalls, logins or other access controls, and contributions that try to will be refused.
- It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube, Google, TikTok, X, Discord, Apple, Adobe or any other
  platform. Platform names are used only to describe compatibility.

## Your responsibilities

- Respect copyright and related rights, and the terms of service of the platforms you use.
- Do not use AETHRYON to redistribute content you have no rights to.
- If you self-host the media API, you are responsible for your instance: restrict access, keep the limits and the
  politeness settings (rate limits, cool-downs), and answer takedown requests.

## Third-party software

The media API relies on [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) (Unlicense) and [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)
(LGPL/GPL depending on the build). They are installed from their official distributions when the image is built and
are not part of this repository.
