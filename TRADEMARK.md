AETHRYON is open-source software. The **code** in this repository is licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The **AETHRYON brand** is not: this policy explains what you may and may not do with it.

## What the brand includes

- the name **AETHRYON** and the product names built on it (AETHRYON Tools, AETHRYON Desktop, AETHRYON Setup…);
- the **owl** (master head, variants and expressions), the **logotype**, app icons and favicons;
- illustrations, the launch film, sounds, screenshots and other media in `assets/`, `packages/brand/` and
  `apps/*/public` or `apps/*/src/assets` (except third-party files that carry their own license, such as fonts).

All rights in these brand assets are reserved by the AETHRYON project owner.

## What you may do without asking

- Use the name **AETHRYON** to refer to the official project in a factual way: articles, reviews, tutorials,
  "compatible with AETHRYON", links to the official website or repository.
- Show unmodified official logos to illustrate such a reference (for example in a blog post about AETHRYON).
- Build and run the code privately, and keep the brand in **unmodified** builds you run for yourself.

## What requires written permission

- Distributing a **modified** version (fork, extension, app, Shortcut, bot or hosted service) under the AETHRYON name
  or with the owl or logotype.
- Using the brand in a product name, domain name, social account, app-store listing or package name.
- Selling merchandise or any paid product using the brand.
- Any use suggesting that the AETHRYON project endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with you.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the code license. Before you distribute one, **rename it and replace the brand assets**:
remove the owl, the logotype and the name AETHRYON from the user interface, icons, store listings and documentation
(a factual mention such as "based on AETHRYON" is fine). Use your own endpoints and your own extension key.
